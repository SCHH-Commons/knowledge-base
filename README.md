# SCHH Knowledge Base  

The **SCHH Knowledge Base** is a curated collection of community-sourced information about Sun City Hilton Head (SCHH) and its surrounding areas. This repository serves as the foundation for the SCHH AI Chatbot and other tools created within the **SCHH Commons** initiative, aimed at improving access to local resources and enhancing the community experience.

## **Purpose**
The knowledge base is designed to:
- Provide a centralized source of information about SCHH amenities, clubs, events, and services.
- Assist in building tools like the SCHH AI Chatbot, which makes this information easily accessible.
- Foster collaboration by enabling community members to contribute, refine, and expand the available data.

## **Scope**
The repository includes:
- General information about Sun City Hilton Head and the local area.
- Details about community resources, amenities, and activities.
- Practical tips and FAQs for residents and prospective residents.

While every effort is made to ensure the accuracy and relevance of the content, contributions are sourced from the community, and the information may not always be comprehensive or error-free.

## **Contributing**
We welcome contributions from the SCHH community! Whether it’s adding new information, correcting existing content, or suggesting improvements, your input helps make the knowledge base more useful for everyone. Please review our contribution guidelines before submitting changes.

## **Disclaimer**
The SCHH Knowledge Base is a collaborative effort and does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of the information provided. Use this resource as a starting point, but always verify critical details independently when needed.
