## Sun City Election: NR and ANR Information Session

Tuesday, September 16 at 6 p.m. at Pinckney Hall, Edisto Room
Sun City Hilton Head’s Annual election will be held November 3-7. Candidates for 35 Neighborhood Representative (NR) and Alternate Neighborhood Representative (ANR) positions will be on the ballots.

To find out more about the NR and ANR positions, join the Election subcommittee’s information session on Tuesday, September 16 at 6 p.m. at Pinckney Hall, Edisto Room. During the information sessions you will learn the responsibilities of these positions and their importance to Sun City. You will have an opportunity to ask questions about what is expected if you choose to serve.

NR/ANR applications can be found on the Election page of the community website or picked up at Palmetto Commons. The deadline for applications is Wednesday, October 8 at 3 p.m. 

## Golf Course Policy Change on Shared Golf Carts

Recently, the Community Association announced that Sun City golf courses will reinstate a policy of allowing no more than two carts per tee time. Hidden Cypress and Argent Lakes Golf Courses reinstated this policy on September 1. Okatie Creek Golf Course will enforce this policy beginning Wednesday, October 1.

## Update: Lakehouse Maintenance Entrance and Lobby Closure

The Lakehouse lobby and main entrance remains closed for renovations. On Thursday, September 11 through Monday, September 15 the hallway leading to the meeting rooms will be closed for flooring installation. During that time, the Lakehouse ballroom will be closed for flooring repairs. Residents are asked to avoid the area. 

Access to the building remains limited. Residents may enter through the Lakehouse outdoor pool gate for access to the indoor pool, gym and locker rooms. Please note, this entrance does not have push-to-open access for those who need a handicap accessible entrance.

Residents who need to use the push-to-open entrances should use the Purrysburg or Hidden Cypress fitness centers or pools.

Thank you for your patience as crews work diligently to complete this renovation.
