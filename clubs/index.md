### Bid Whist Club

A Fun and Easy Card Game to Play. Bid Whist is a variant, played in the United States, of the classic card game, whist. As indicated by the name, bid whist adds a bidding element to the game that is not present in classic whist. It is generally accepted that the game of bridge came from the game of whist.

### Canasta Club

The Canasta Club meets Thursdays and Sundays from 12:30 to 4 PM in the Yemassee Craft Center Card Room across from Purrysburg Fitness Center. We welcome all residents who play any form of Canasta. If you have a home game, bring your team and join the fun. There are many variations of Canasta played by our members. We offer lessons to members for Super Samba on the 2nd Thursday of every month.

### Contract Bridge Club

All games are played at the Yemassee Craft Center. Tuesday Game 12:45 pm, Wednesday Game 8:30 am., and Thursday Game 8:30 am. are open to all using Chicago scoring. Friday partners, Chicago scoring 6:30 pm.

### Cribbage Club

The Cribbage club encourages players to test their skills in a friendly atmosphere of competition each week.

### Dominoes Club

Welcome! We play Mexican Train Dominoes. Don't know how to play? No problem. Join us any Monday and we will teach you.

### Euchre Club

### Gin Rummy Club

Brighten your Tuesday evening by spending the evening with the Gin Rummy Club enjoying some good company, conversation and a fun game of cards. 

### Hearts Club

### Okatie Creek Duplicate Bridge Club

We welcome all bridge players interested in a friendly competitive bridge game. We are a sanctioned ACBL club awarding Master Points. Lessons are available for beginners, intermediate and advanced players. We play on Mondays 1–4:15 p.m., Tuesdays 9 a.m.–12:15 p.m. (2 games per month are team games) and Saturdays 9 a.m.–12:15 p.m. Hands are pre-dealt. Results and analysis of hands played are posted on the website following games. If you need a partner, check out our website. Check the webpage listed above for details about lessons and special events.

### Pinochle Club

We play double deck for Move-Up and Non Move-Up games (Move-Up Games consist of changing partners and winners advance to the next table... Non Move-up consists of staying on one table and playing with your same group throughout the night). Move-up game begins at 6 p.m.

### All About Art

We invite you to join us and share our love of art. Regardless of your skill level, there will be others who share your interest. Meet other artists, learn a new art media or improve your skills through instructor-led art classes that are offered in our well-equipped studio. Click on the website link below to see what is going on now in our club.

### Amateur Radio Club

Welcome to the Sun City Hilton Head Amateur Radio Club.

### Avant Gardeners

Welcome to Avant Gardeners! The purpose of this club is to promote interest in the art and practice of gardening and the preservation of our natural environment. To this end our monthly meetings feature a guest speaker addressing issues of interest to Lowcountry gardeners. Master Gardeners are available to answer questions at the Q & A table.

### Bon Appétit Cooking Club

The purpose of this Club is to serve as a social get-together for members to meet and learn different types of cooking and short-cuts to cooking.

### Book Club, Chapter I

"Chapter 1 opens the book, the ending not yet seen." Presentation of the book each month is the responsibility of one member, and all members are encouraged to assume this responsibility on a periodic basis. Monthly book discussions/presentations do not follow a prescribed format as the presenting member uses his/her creativity to engage members in the review/discussion process.

### Book Club, Chapter II

Join us in our book discussions as we further our understanding of differing viewpoints and explore human nature. Each member is responsible to be a presenter, or co-presenter, rotating basis every two years.

### Book Club, Chapter III

We meet on the third Wednesday of each month at 1 p.m. in the Coosaw and New River Rooms of Hidden Cypress.

### Book Club, Chapter IV

Book Club IV meets the first Thursday of every month to discuss and dissect books to find their meaning and universal truths.

### Book Club, Chapter V

We explore American history from an African American perspective. We meet at 3:00 pm on the first and fourth Wednesdays in the Bayside Room Lake House.

### Ceramics Club

We do Hand-Building, Wheel-Throwing & Sculpting with high fire & low fire clays, as well as Hand Painting on green ware & bisque. We have mentors & classes to help NEWBEES get started. We fire pieces in our 5 kilns twice a week as needed.

### Coin Club of Sun City

Unless indicated otherwise, all meetings are at Hidden Cypress in the Santee River Room on the 3rd Thursday of the month at 7:00 p.m.

### Computer Club

We are much more than computers. We are about communications and technology, using smartphones, tablets and social media. We teach how to USE this technology with applications to stay connected, entertain and learn. Explore your heritage, view the universe or something closer to home, like your new computer, iPad, tablet, digital TV or smartphone.

### Glasscrafters Club

Sign up sheets for the next month's class offerings are available on the first Monday of each month at the Yemassee Craft Center Studio.

### Karaoke Club

We proudly offer dancing, singing, sing-a-longs, laughing, hugging and great conversations with super people plus guaranteed exceptional drinks & nibblers since it is a BYOE (Bring Your Own Everything)! Consider this your personal invitation!

### Okatie Farmers Club

The Okatie Farmers Club is dedicated to the cultivation of vegetables and herbs. We have more than 300 garden plots - and with a growing season of 12 months, members are able to "farm" all year 'round. 

### Photography Club

Visit our Studio in the Crafts Center -All Skill Levels are Welcome Check Us Out!

### Sew What Stitches & Crafts Club

The Sew What Stitches & Craft Club is dedicated to encouraging our member's interests in sewing and crafts. We offer a variety of classes in quilting, knitting, card making, papercraft, basketry, beading and much more.

### Stamp Collectors Club

This Sun City Hilton Head Stamp Club is made up of stamp collectors and philatelic enthusiasts. We meet once a month, to discuss, exhibit, and trade postage stamps and related philatelic items from the United States and the rest of the world. We occasionally will schedule speakers to share and discuss their areas of collecting expertise. Monthly meetings are open to residents and friends of Sun City Hilton Head.

### Sun City Bird Club

The Club is organized to provide education for members in the field identification and behavior of local bird life. Emphasis is placed on recognition of various species, habitat improvement and preservation. New members are always welcome!

### Woodworkers & Modelmakers Guild

The Guild's Workshop is located at 841 Red Dam Road. The shop is open 9 a.m.-5 p.m., Monday, Wednesday & Friday and Saturday 10 a.m.-4 p.m. We have special interest groups (SIGs) for model railroading, wood carving, wood turning, and woodchicks.

### Car Club

The Sun City Car Club, established in 2002, now represents over 190 members. We are a social organization dedicated to promoting all aspects of the “car hobby”. Collecting, repairing, restoring, racing and watching. We are not dedicated to any one single marque. Owning a car is not a requirement to be a member.

This SC Chartered Club encourages responsible pet ownership, sponsors educational and training opportunities, plans activities for dogs and their owners, along with exchange of ideas, information and personal experiences with all kinds of pets.

The purpose of the "Eat Smart, Live Longer" club is to educate and motivate members to eat a whole foods, plant-strong diet so they can improve their lives and avoid or minimize common chronic health conditions.

### Luncheon Club I

Volunteer Hostesses select the restaurant and menu and advise the secretary who publishes the information in SunSations and prepares a flier to be distributed or e-mailed to members. At some meetings, the hostesses arrange for a program, such as a fashion show, for the enjoyment of the members.

### Money Talks Club

The purpose of the Money Talks Club is to provide an unbiased educational platform for members to learn about financial matters.

### The Qi Club

Focuses on the internal arts of qigong, meditation, tai chi, yoga, tapping and community drumming. Join us for healthy, fun exercises and activities to bring your life back into balance.

### Roadrunners Motorcycle Club

The Roadrunners Motorcycle Club is open to all residents of Sun City Hilton Head who are motorcycle enthusiasts, whether or not they currently ride. Our membership is over 140 adult bikers and their partners riding all kinds of bikes, from two-wheel scooters, to all makes of cruisers, to trikes, Can-Am's, and Sling Shots.

### Ballroom Dance Club

Welcomes ballroom dancers. Ballroom dance events occur on Fridays, 6:45 until 9:45 pm, Pinckney Hall, beginning with a dance lesson taught by Fred Astaire Dance Studio instructors, followed by social dancing. Dress code is no shorts or jeans. The lessons feature different dances throughout the year, usually teaching the same dance for three consecutive Fridays, i.e. waltz for three Fridays, followed by cha cha for three Fridays, etc.

### Line Dance Club

The purpose of the club is to promote line dancing as an enjoyable form of exercise and fun among all interested members of Sun City.

### Music Guild

The Music Guild is the Chartered Club to develop and advance all music interests within Sun City Hilton Head, whether singing or playing an instrument. There are nine music interest groups: Chimers, Sun City Jazz Band, Mountain Strings, Sun City Harmonizers (a cappella), Raparees (an Irish singing group), Sun City Strummers, Sun City Singers, Sun Tones (women's a cappella), and Sun City Ukulele Band. You will find information on all these groups by clicking Music Guild above.

### Sun City Chorus and Orchestra

The Sun City Chorus and Orchestra is a musical organization that achieves excellence through education, enthusiasm and good times under the musical direction of Art Hansen.

### Sun City Theatre

Provides an opportunity for dramatic expression and participation in all phases of theatre activities for the pleasure of our members, friends, and community. Promote opportunities for learning the craft and art of acting and directing through theatre workshops.

### Sun City Squares

Square dancing provides important physical, mental and social benefits. It's a good physical workout and the concentration required to respond to a rapid sequence of calls is an excellent mental stimulation. Club members dance as couples. The bonds of friendship that form between couples during lessons and dancing are very strong and carry over to other social activities. Above all, it's fun!

### Sundancers

Sundancers mission is to instill a great love of dance, while inspiring self-confidence, discipline and respect for the performing arts. Knowledge is shared and encouragement provided to everyone with a desire to learn while cultivating camaraderie and developing friendships. 

### Synchronettes Club

The Synchronettes enjoy synchronized swimming each Saturday from 9-11 a.m. at the indoor pool of Purrysburg Fitness Center. We also meet on Wednesdays from 1-3 p.m. when we learn new skills and techniques. The members do fitness warm-ups and practice synchronized swimming skills and routines to music. It's great exercise and lots of fun! Membership is open to residents who are skilled in the basic elements of swimming. We invite you to come visit us at a Saturday practice. We love new members. Thank you for your support at our shows.

### Bicycle Club

Our 250 members are cyclists ranging in skill from beginner to hardcore devotee. Our goal is to make members aware of cycling opportunities appropriate to their skill level. These include day rides in and out of Sun City and overnight trips to highly acclaimed regional sites. Club meetings provide an opportunity to socialize with others having a similar interest, share riding experiences, and hear guest speakers on cycling related subjects.

### Billiard Club

The Sun City Billiard Club meets in the Billiard Room of the Yemassee Craft Center and players of all levels are welcome.

### Bocce Club

Our club is currently one of the larger in Sun City with over 1800 members and 49 Neighborhood and Group leagues.  Residents may reserve court time through the Chelsea system.  To register, please call the Logo Building at 843-705-4034.  A daily schedule is posted at the Bocce courts and is available online through the Chelsea system. Community bocce equipment for residents and guests is kept in an unlocked storage tote located near the picnic table at the courts.  

### Couples Golf

Sun City Couples Golf is a group dedicated to fun and social interaction mixed in with golf competition.

### Croquet Club

The Sun City Croquet Club plays several variations of United States Croquet Association (USCA) croquet including: 6-Wicket 14 Point & 26 Point Croquet, Golf Croquet, 9-Wicket (Backyard) Croquet, and Association Rules Croquet.

### Fishing & Hunting Club

The Fishing & Hunting Club promotes good fellowship, enhances knowledge and enjoyment of fish and game activities, and follows sound environmental practices.

### Horseshoe Club

Horseshoes are normally played Thursday 1 pm and Saturdays, 10 am-Noon, New River Sports Park. There is a set of shoes left out for anyone to practice at their convenience.

### Kayak Club of Sun City

Sun City is fortunate to have over 30 public kayaking/boating access points within a 30 mile radius. This provides us with miles and miles of fresh and salt water to enjoy. We try to have club paddles (trips) every month. Some are "short" paddles in the 5-8 mile range which are suitable for members with all skill levels and almost any kind of kayak. It is very common to have solo women and men paddlers on our outings and there is always plenty of help to load and unload kayaks.

### Men’s Nine Hole Golf Club

Our club offers an opportunity for men who enjoy playing nine holes of golf, to play with guaranteed tee times each Monday morning, alternating between Okatie Creek, Hidden Cypress, and Argent Lakes golf courses.

### Men’s 18 Hole Golf Association

The Club provides the male golfers of Sun City with a vehicle for competition and social contact. We compete every Wednesday, alternating amongst the SCHH golf courses. We primarily compete as teams in flights based on handicap so that you are competing as a group against golfers with handicaps similar to your own.

### Pickleball Club

Join our club to attend our social events and to play in leagues which are offered in the Fall and Spring.

### Silver Striders

Silver Striders meet 2-3 times a week to run or walk together and socialize.  The club also provides an opportunity to meet new people, become part of a community, and establish accountability to oneself.  The health benefits of walking and running are numerous, including improving fitness, relieving stress and increasing energy levels. Monthly activities include a group walk on a Hilton Head beach and a special walk to interesting locations in the area, followed by lunch. Often a group of walkers and runners participate in locally organized events.

### Softball Club

Our 560+ members include softball players ranging in skill from rank beginner to the proverbial softball junkie. The Club's goal is to provide members the opportunity to enjoy softball in a forum appropriate to their skill level and preferred competitive intensity; while emphasizing fair, balanced, and safe play. The Club also provides opportunities to engage socially with others having a similar interest in the sport.

### Sun City Swim Club

Whether you can swim 25 yards or 3,000 yards, we have a workout group for you! Coach-lead workouts help Novice, Fitness and Advanced swimmers improve their strokes while having fun with a friendly social group. Visit our club webpage for workout/days times. Come watch a workout and see what we're all about. 

### Sun City Hilton Head Golf Club

To promote the enjoyment of the game of golf in sun city by uniting and communicating efficiently and effectively with all golfers and golf associations.

### Table Tennis Club

We invite you to come join us for a friendly game of table tennis, the perfect sport for active adults as it helps maintain coordination, agility and quickness, all in a climate-controlled environment.

### Tennis Association

The Sun City Tennis Association (SCTA) is committed to creating, maintaining, and protecting an exceptional and collegial tennis environment for its members and guests. We will do this through ethical conduct, visionary planning for the future, active and creative communication, and prudent, fiscally sound business decisions.

### Volleyball Club

The Volleyball Club is a co-ed group that attempts to have fun and develop the skills of those involved. Indoor volleyball has a charge, since we're using an offsite facility. Outdoor and water volleyball are included in the annual membership.

### Women’s 9 Hole Golf Club

We play every Monday morning unless the courses are closed. Tee times start at 8:00. Generally, for the 1st and 3rd Mondays of each month, games are played as a fun team competition. The 2nd and 4th Mondays are social play. Play alternates between Okatie Creek, Hidden Cypress and Argent Lakes Golf Courses.

### Women’s Golf Association

We play 18 holes each Thursday and alternate among Okatie Creek, Hidden Cypress and Argent Lakes. All handicaps are welcome (maximum of 36 used in competition).
