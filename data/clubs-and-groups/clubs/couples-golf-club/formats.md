# Couples Golf Format Descriptions

> ALL SCORES ARE POSTED TO GHIN BY GOLF GENIUS UNLESS OTHERWISE NOTED 
ALWAYS RECORD GROSS SCORES - GOLF GENIUS CALCULATES NET SCORES 

## ONE BEST GROSS AND ONE BEST NET 

This is a 4 person event.  
All players play their own ball and record gross scores. The team score adds together one best gross score and one best net score of the  foursome. 
The low gross and low net cannot be counted from the same person. If the gross score is counted, the next low net will be used for the  team score. 
This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% 

## BEST NET OF A/C and BEST NET OF B/D

This is a 4 person event. 
The low index player in couple #1 is the A player and their partner is B. 
The low index player in couple #2 is the C player and their partner is D. 
All players play their won ball and record gross scores.  
Scoring counts the one best net of player A & C added to the one best net of player B & D 
This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% 

## ODD/EVEN – 3 NET ON ODD HOLES AND 2 NET ON EVEN HOLES

This is a 4 person event. 
All players play their own ball and record gross scores. The team score adds together the 3 best net scores on odd holes, and 2 best net  scores on even holes. 
This event flighted and handicapped at 100% 

## STEP ASIDE SCRAMBLE

This is a 4 person event. 
All players tee off. The team decides which tee shot to use. The person whose ball is chosen “steps aside” and doesn’t play the next shot.  The process is repeated on each shot through the hole until the ball is in the hole. Three players hit each shot with the person who played  the previous shot sitting out. 
You may move the ball up to one club length from where the selected ball lies, no closer to the hole, staying in the same condition (fairway  stays in fairway, rough stays in rough, and penalty stays in the penalty area). 
This event is flighted only – scores are not posted. 

## STABLEFORD SCORING – COUNT TWO BEST STABLEFORD SCORES

This is a 4 person event. 
All players play their own ball and record gross scores. 
Stableford points are awarded to each NET score as follows: 
NET BOGIE – 1 POINT 
NET PAR – 2 POINTS 
NET BIRDIE – 4 POINTS 
NET EAGLE – 6 POINTS 
In this event, high scores win. The two highest stableford points are added together for the team score. 
If a score exceeds NET BOGIE, pick up the ball because no Stableford points will be awarded. Record the expected gross score for this  player. 
This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% 

# COUPLE SCRAMBLE

This is a two person event 
Both players tee off. Select the best drive and then both players hit a ball from that spot. Then select the best ball from those two results  and both players hit the next shot from there. Keep repeating, both players hitting, including on the green, until the ball is in the hole. You  may move the ball up to one club length from where it lies, no closer to the hole and in the same condition (fairway stays in fairway, rough  stays in rough, hazard stays in hazard). 
This event is flighted only - scores are not posted 

## 3 BEST NET OF 4

This is a 4 person event. 
All players play their own ball and record gross scores. The 3 best net scores are added together for the team score. This event is flighted and handicapped at 100%

## SHAMBLE

This is a 4 person event. 
All players tee off and select one drive. From that point everyone plays their own ball through the green. (there are always 4 balls in play). Record the gross score for each player.  
The selected tee shot may be moved one club length from where it lies, no closer to the hole, staying in the same condition (fairway stays in  fairway, rough stays in rough, and hazard stays in hazard) 
This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% - scores are not posted 

## I NET ON PAR 5s, 2 NETS ON PAR 4s, 3 NETS ON PAR 3s

This is a 4 person event. 
All players play their own ball and record gross scores. Team score counts one best net score on par 5s, two best net scores on par 4s, and 3  best net scores on par 3s 
This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% 

## BEST NET OF COUPLE

This is a two person event. 
Each person in the pair plays their own ball and records gross score. The team score is the one best net score of the couple. This event is flighted and handicapped at 100% 

## COMBINED NET SCORE

This is a two person event. 
Each player plays their own ball and gross scores are recorded. The team score is the combined net of the twosome. This flight is handicapped and flighted at 100% 

## 3,3,3 and 6,6,6

These are two person events where three different formats are played – scramble, best ball, and alternate shot.  In a 9 hole event: 
Play the first 3 holes in a couple scramble (recording one score for the team) 
Play the second 3 holes as best ball - each play your own ball and record your gross score (best net score counts) Play the last 3 holes as modified alternate shot (recording one score for the team).  
In an 18 hole event, the same formats will be followed, but you will play 6 holes of each.

- Scramble – both players tee off. Then select the best drive and both players hit from that spot. Then select the best ball from that  outcome and both play from that spot. Keep repeating, both players hitting until the ball is holed out. Once a ball is selected to play, you may move it within one club length, no closer to the hole, in the same condition. 
- Best ball – both players play their own ball all the way through the hole and record gross scores. The best net of the couple  counts as the team score. 
- Modified alternate shot – Both players tee off, select the best ball and alternate shots from there, beginning with the person who  did not hit the tee shot. The ball selected must be played throughout the hole – no switching balls. 

This event is flighted and only the best ball section is handicapped. 
Scores are not posted 

## MODIFIED ALTERNATE SHOT

Both players tee off and select the best ball. Alternate shots from there, beginning with the person who did not hit the tee shot. The ball  selected must be played throughout the hole – no switching balls. 
On the next hole, start the process again with both teeing off and then alternating shots from the selected bll. This event is flighted only - Scores are not posted 

## MODIFIED BEST BALL

Both players tee off, then switch and play each other’s tee shot. Continue to play that ball throughout the hole. The team score is the best  net score of the couple. You must play the ball hit from the tee box, no switching balls. 
If a player hits a tee shot that results in a penalty stroke, the partner who plays the ball in takes that penalty. This event is flighted and handicapped 100% 
Scores are not posted.
