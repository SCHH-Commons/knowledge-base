# Sun City Tennis Association

As a resident of Sun City, you are entitled to use the tennis facility and play on any of the 16 courts on site. You can participate in the clinics and lessons offered by our tennis professionals. You may also join in the “come and Play” Round Robin at 7:30 am (please arrive at 7:15) on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, no registration needed.

After completing the required training, you may also use the ball machine, situated on the South side. Ball machine training takes place on the first Saturday of the month.

Tennis courts are reserved using the Chelsea system. Contact the Logo building staff to get set up with a username and password. You will also need to contact the Logo building to register for all clinics and ball machine training.

If you are looking to meet players at your level, use the “Need an Opponent” tab on Chelsea. You can specify when you would like to play, and whether you play singles or doubles!

Whether you are a seasoned player or a beginner, if you want to add to your play, you are encouraged to become a member of the Sun City Tennis Association (SCTA), a Chartered club that provides multiple opportunities for you to enjoy even more tennis! Some of these include:

- Sponsored tournaments including Mix and Match, a 4 day tennis extravaganza
- IntraClub – weekly, at level, competitive league play
- Rookie League – for those starting competitive play
- New to Tennis social play for beginners
- Annual SCTA Club Championships
- World Team Tennis – a seasonal at level mini league
- Social events throughout the year
- USTA League play
- Ability to reserve seasonal courts (noon-8:30) for your group of players, by participating in the lotteries held three times each year.

Need help getting started? Whether you want to just get out and play, or join SCTA, one of the Tennis Ambassadors will help you get started and provide you with all you need to know.

If you know your USTA or Club rating contact your Ambassador listed below.
If you’re unsure of your playing level or who to contact, reach out to Fiona Harwood, the SCTA Ambassador Coordinator (harwoodusa@yahoo.com) for assistance.

The staff in LOGO Building are also here to help, stop by or give them a call.

## Tennis Lottery

### What is the “Lottery”?

Every tennis facility has some sort of program to provide “season courts” to their members. It usually involve s some kind of fee, and it set up such that 1, 2, or 3 courts are reserved for a group of people for a given duration. Sun City Tennis Association (SCTA) provides this service to members with no additional fee. Season courts (also known as Social Groups) are available to members for each of the three seasons that we manage:

Approx Lottery Date (see calendar)|Jan 9|May 8|Sep 10 

However, given nearly 21,000 residents on site, and approx 750 members of SCTA, we have to limit the number of courts people can reserve for their groups each week. We attempt to fairly distribute our limited courts fo r members to reserve in advance, such that there will always be sufficient courts available for everyone, including leagues and just getting courts through Chelsea.

SCTA has been given permission to reserve season courts for our members, but only in the afternoons. We se t up a “Lottery” to help ensure courts are distributed as evenly as possible, limiting people to a certain number of
“pre-placement” opportunities per week. “Pre-placement” refers to getting courts assigned to people PRIOR t o Chelsea determining placement with its own prioritization algorithm. Therefore, separate controls are require d for these pre-placed (lottery) courts.

The controls we use include running a meeting called the ‘Seasonal Lottery’ for all those wanting season courts. It is scheduled a few weeks before the start of each season. At this meeting, each group leader receives a ticke t and when his/her number is called, indicates the day, time, and courts desired for their season courts.

Rules for the lottery are published on our web page (www.sctatennis.com) several months before each season. They include special rules such as limiting 3-court groups to 4 pm or later on weekdays in spring and fall, and  a discussion regarding “lottery points” accumulated for each member of a group or league. Like routine Chelse a limitations designed to prevent over-use, this system allows us to evenly distribute our limited number of “preplacement” courts to an ever-expanding membership.

The “Lottery” meeting involves only Social Groups, as leagues are placed on the schedule before anyone pick s their season courts. However, any kind of “pre-placement” involves “lottery points”, with limits applied an d managed by a season setup and scheduling committee such that we can ensure fair distribution.

Two separate limits apply such that pre-placement courts (leagues & season courts acquired via the lottery) ar e distributed fairly.
- One limit restricts members such that their CAM # may appear on no more than 6 total registrations.
- A second limit determines an adjusted total expected plays per week in prime time as a result o f pre-placement. This value is calculated by multiplying each pre-placed reservation by its weightin g factor (0.7 for groups, 0.15 for USTA, 0.45 for intra/inter) and summing the products. The objective o f this limit (2.6) is to ensure equitable distribution of our courts in prime time. These “Lottery Points” ar e not to be confused with “Chelsea points”. (See online Policy & Procedures manual, Section 9).

Note: No Lottery or Chelsea points are awarded to teams/groups playing at 5:30 pm or later.
