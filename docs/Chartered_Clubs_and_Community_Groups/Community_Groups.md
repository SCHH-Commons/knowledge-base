Each quarter, members select a local nonprofit charity to receive their collective donation of over $18,000. In less than an hour, they hear three member presentations, vote, and each writes a $100 tax-deductible check directly to the chosen charity. By pooling resources, the group shines as a beacon of light and hope for many in need. Becoming a member is simple and hassle-free—no committee work, solicitation, or sales activities required. Join us to observe or become part of this philanthropic initiative!  

**Meetings:** 5 p.m. in Pinckney Hall. Registration and socializing at 4:30 p.m. See *SunSations* for details.

Wreaths Across America is a national project to place fresh evergreen remembrance wreaths on veterans’ graves each December.
