# Argent Lakes Pub and Pizzeria

a.k.a. "AL's Pub"

Argent Lakes Pub and Pizzeria opened in the fall of 2020 and is part of the Jameson’s Charhouse family of restaurants. Located on the North Side of Sun City, Argent Lakes Pub and Pizzeria offers an abundance of outdoor seating, lounge seating, bar seating along with community tables for all to enjoy.

Our full-service bar features tap, bottle and local craft beers. Our fresh bar concept features specialty cocktails such as the smash’d and crush’d cocktails. Freshly squeezed juices enhance our “crush’d” and muddled fresh mint with fresh squeezed lemon make our classic bourbon “smash’d” a favorite for all.

Like our cocktails we take great pride in our pizzas. We only source family-owned California tomatoes for our red sauce. Our mozzarella cheese has a hint of Buffalo milk for extra richness. Our hand sliced artesian sweet sausage will leave you wanting more. In addition to our pizzas, we offer shared appetizers for all to enjoy. We will always source local and regional whenever possible and are committed to using only fresh ingredients in everything we make for you.

Our message is simple: drink – laugh – share – repeat
