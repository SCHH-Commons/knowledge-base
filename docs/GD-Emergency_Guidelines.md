# Sun City Hilton Head Emergency Guidelines for Residential Emergency Permits and Inspections

**Please be advised, the responsibility for obtaining permits and re-build of residential property is that of the homeowner.**

If Beaufort and Jasper counties are declared disaster areas following a hurricane or other natural disaster, Sun City Emergency Guidelines will take effect. That means that Sun City’s design and new construction guidelines will be modified so homeowners can get** quicker approval to restore their homes to what they once were. All rebuilding approvals will also be subject to Beaufort and Jasper counties’ permitting process. Check the county websites for more information:

- Beaufort County: [http://www.beaufortcountysc.gov](http://www.beaufortcountysc.gov)
- Jasper County: [http://www.jaspercountysc.gov](http://www.jaspercountysc.gov)

## Plans for Architectural Review Board Guidelines

In case of a major disaster, the Modifications Committee has adopted procedures to expedite project review and approval.

- In situations where the property is going to be reconstructed virtually identical to the original home, including previously approved modifications, homeowners will have to sign and submit a Declaration Form for review.
- Residents do need approval for any new additions or changes.

In case Sun City Hilton Head is not fully accessible in the aftermath of a hurricane or natural disaster, Modifications Committee members will not be required to visit each site. Instead, members may review and approve design plans electronically sent to them at off-property locations. As always, the Modifications Committee and Community Association management intend to work closely and cooperatively with each landowner to restore a safe and secure living environment.

## Beaufort and Jasper Counties Rebuilding Permitting Process

All rebuilding will be subject to Beaufort or Jasper county building codes and permitting process. The appropriate county will inspect all severely damaged properties and issue a status card depending on their damage assessment.

The approval process includes three levels of necessary actions to be taken, dependent on the amount of damage experienced and the desires of the resident when rebuilding. The county will issue a green, yellow or red Emergency Repair Permitting After Disaster placard for damage less than or greater than 50 percent. For additional information check the county websites:

- Beaufort County: [http://www.beaufortcountysc.gov](http://www.beaufortcountysc.gov)
- Jasper County: [http://www.jaspercountysc.gov](http://www.jaspercountysc.gov)


## Level 1 – A Declaration Form (an Affidavit) signed by the property owner is required and

the Reviewing Body will require no prior approval.

To remove trees or for minor repairs, homeowners must submit before and after pictures of the property and notify the Modifications Department in writing of the repairs to properly update files. These repairs include:
- Removing trees that have fallen on or across homeowner’s property or homes, or are leaning more than 30 degrees
- Minor roof repairs involving less than 50 percent of the total roof and garage surface. The roofing materials must match the original color and texture
- Replacing broken windows with those that match the original window
- Replacing less than 50 percent of all exterior wall materials with matching materials and repainting with pre-approved or an identical paint color
- Replacing any damaged exterior doors or garage doors and repainting with a pre-approved or an identical paint color

## Level 3 – Approval by the County and SCHH Reviewing Body is required.

Repairing extensive damage or rebuilding.

In cases where the property needs to be substantially rebuilt and the property owner decides to change the design, size or exterior materials, the traditional application approval process will be required. This includes all proposed property improvements other than those listed above and changes such as raising the ground floor elevation, changing exterior wall material and color, or expanding or shrinking landscaping.
