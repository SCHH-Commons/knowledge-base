# WHO TO CALL FIRST IN AN EMERGENCY: Hardeeville and Jasper County 

Neighbors, This is a list of local emergency numbers, and the first contact you should make if you need 
assistance.  For additional detailed information, contact the appropriate website.  The links in this email are 
active.  If you save the document to your phone or computer, you can just click on them if you need them.  For 
non-emergency Sun City Assistance, please refer to the Community Directory.  It is published in every issue of 
SunSations Magazine.  
 
FIRE AND MEDICAL EMERGENCIES: 9-1-1  
Jasper County Lift & Assist: (843) 726-7519  
Utilities: 
Dominion Energy (Gas Leak or equipment damage) – call from a safe place away from the leak 
800-815-0083 
https://www.dominionenergy.com/south-carolina/report-outage-or-emergency 
 
 
Palmetto Electric:   For downed wires, Call 9-1-1 immediately –  
Stay at least 30 feet away from downed lines – consider them energized and dangerous 
Outages: Check the main breaker first 
Call PowerTouch: 1-866-445-5551 
https://www.palmetto.coop/report-power-outage
