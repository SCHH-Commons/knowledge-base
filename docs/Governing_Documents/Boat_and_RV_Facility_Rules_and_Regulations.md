# Boat/RV Facility Rules and Regulations

The Sun City Hilton Head Boat/RV Facility is a Community Association-owned amenity provided to eligible community residents. This amenity is intended to be utilized as a parking area, not to be used as a storage facility.

Any infraction of the Community Association’s Governing Documents, including these rules and regulations, may result in fines, loss of parking and campsite privileges, loss of community privileges, and other enforcement actions that are warranted, including units being towed from the facility. The Community Association will levy all fines per the Community Association Schedule of Fees and Community Rules, and any other enforcement actions that are warranted as stated in the Schedule of Fees and Community Rules.

The term “unit” will include all boats, RVs (all classes and sizes), all travel trailers, specialty hobby trailers, personal-use utility trailers, vehicle trailers, motorcycle trailers, and all other trailers.

These rules and regulations are subject to change by the Sun City Hilton Head Board of Directors as deemed appropriate.

## GENERAL RULES, POLICIES AND PROCEDURES

1. A fee of $100 payable to the Community Association is required at the time of application. A copy of a valid registration (if applicable) and current insurance must be submitted with the application. The deposit is non-refundable if made after January 2021. Deposits made before January 2021 will be refunded if/when the resident removes their unit from the Boat/RV Facility and notifies the Lot Manager of termination by filling out a form and submitting it.

2. An annual site fee will be charged on the January statement to the resident’s Community Association account for their unit site(s). This charge is based on the resident’s unit type and unit size. Proof of unit registration, if applicable, and unit valid insurance will be requested annually. Refer to the Community Association Schedule of Fees and Community Rules for the current fee amount. A prorated amount of the fee will be refunded when the resident removes their unit from the Boat/RV Facility and notifies the Community Association Lot Manager of termination of agreement.

3. Only one unit is permitted in each site, with the exception of a tow dolly (space permitting) parked behind a RV with the Lot Manager’s approval.

4. No units for resident contractors or resident commercial/business use are permitted to have unit sites in the facility. Signage, including but not limited to, for sale, contractors, businesses or advertising of any kind, is not allowed to be posted on the units or in the Boat/RV Facility. Signage as to rules, events and information for the users of the sites is allowed but must be approved by the Lot Manager before posting.

5. Sheds, storage containers, or any materials, etc. are not permitted on unit sites with the exception of boat storage locker/containers for boat covers/equipment.

6. Units must be “road ready” at all times. The Community Association defines “road ready” as follows:
    - Current valid registration (if applicable) and current valid insurance assigned to the resident owner of the unit(s)
    - Tires are inflated
    - Good condition (both operational and in clean appearance) as determined by Lot Manager
    - Units and/or property in disrepair or non-compliant with the rules set forth in this document will not be permitted to park in the facility. Failure to comply or correct deficiencies within 15 days after written notification may result in a fine and loss of facility privileges. If corrective action is not made within 45 days after written notification, the unit and/or property may be towed from the Boat/RV Facility at the owner’s expense, including moving, towing and storage fees.

7. Each unit must be assigned to a single resident bearer of liability.

8. Parking of additional units on a temporary basis is limited to the resident’s site space dimensions and the Lot Manager’s pre-approval.

9. Open utility trailers shall be empty of cargo, except for a spare tire.

10. Assigned sites are not transferrable between units or residents. Do not park your unit on another site without notifying the Lot Manager. The Lot Manager has the authority to reassign or change an assigned site as needed.

11. A change of a unit is a new unit, for the purpose of a site assignment and decals. Communication to the Lot Manager of a change in unit is required. This may result in the “new” unit being assigned to a different site.

12. Driving is only permitted through the aisles. Do not enter or drive through unoccupied sites. The speed limit within the facility is 10 mph for safety and to keep dust to a minimum.

13. Unit covers shall be constructed to fit your type of unit and constructed of sturdy materials with all corners and edges securely tied down. Drop cloths, tarps and any cover made of plastic, including shrink wrap, are not permitted. Ripped or torn covers are not permitted. Covers should be clean, not showing mold or dirt, and should be stored when not in use. Any exceptions will need the approval of the Lot Manager.

14. Only gray/black water shall be deposited in the dump station holding tanks. No chemicals, fuels,cleaning solutions or pollutants should be deposited into the dump station tanks. Water and the hose at the dump station is “non potable.”

15. The wash bay is to be used for washing and rinsing your boat or RV. Washing/rinsing of any personal vehicles not stored in the Boat/RV Facility is prohibited. Flushing of boat motors is allowed. RV units have priority until 2 p.m.; boats have priority after 2 p.m. Priority does not mean “not allowed” after a certain time. It means if the station is occupied, the person can finish what they are doing, and the priority would then be next, before any others enter the station. No chemicals, fuels, or pollutants should be deposited into the drain or on the ground. Hoses at wash station are “non potable.”

16. Electrical outlets are for the use of small tools and for the charging of batteries. Air conditioners/heaters shall not be operated using this power unless preparing for a trip. Units should not be plugged in all the time, exception being the Class A diesel engine units that require more power. Other smaller diesels do not require full time electrical charging. Trickle chargers at 14 gauge wire are allowed. Only a 12 gauge or larger extension cord, no longer than 50 feet are to be used.

In preparation of a unit for a trip, the unit may be plugged in, 30 amps, for running the refrigerator and A/C or heat no longer than 48 hours. The Lot Manager has the authority to unplug a unit that is plugged in that is in violation of this rule. The Lot Manager will notify the site holder of the violation to the rule by email before unplugging the unit. No response to the Lot Manager’s email notice could result in the unit being unplugged.

Fines or termination of the site agreement could occur as a result of not complying with these rules/ guidelines.

A reduced nightly campsite rate of $15 is available for trip preparation or to complete significant repairs or maintenance. Occupancy of the unit is not allowed. Maximum of four nights or longer based on availability and prior approval by the Boat and RV Yard Committee. Contact campsite reservations at (843) 705-3433 to reserve a campsite.

17. This facility is not equipped with trash receptacles or dumpsters. Trash/debris of any kind shall not be left on the premises. Failure to comply could result in sanctions levied against the violators. It is the resident’s responsibility to keep their site clean and free of debris.

18. Due to stringent storm water quality guidelines enforced by South Carolina Department of Health and Environmental Control (DHEC), repairs to a unit, such as replacing hydraulic brakes, oil changes, repairs to drive trains or motors, etc., are not permitted. No work is to be done on units where liquid (other than drinking water) could be discharged onto the ground. While performing authorized repairs, do not leave anything on the ground unattended or overnight. Should liquids
(other than drinking water) be drained or spilled onto the ground, the resident should notify the Lot Manager. Residents may be assessed for any and all costs associated with the cleanup, including fines levied by any government agency as a result of contamination of the site.

19. Should you be unable to safely repair your unit in its parking site, you may temporarily move it to another location with the following conditions. If you only require electrical power, then move to site F-0 (temporary location, 48-hour limit). If you require water and/or sewer service, or extra space then call the campground reservation phone (843) 705-3433, and state that you are in need of a campsite for maintenance. This site is free until sunset. If your unit stays overnight in a campsite, you must contact campsite reservation to gain site permission and to pay the nightly Maintenance fee NO overnight occupancy allowed. If you are going to stay overnight in the unit, campsite reservations must be notified and an appropriate campsite fee is charged.

A reduced nightly campsite rate of $15 is available for trip preparation or to complete significant repairs or maintenance. Occupancy of the unit is not allowed. Maximum of four nights or longer based on availability and prior approval by the Boat and RV Yard Committee. Contact campsite reservations at (843) 705-3433 to reserve a campsite.

20. For safety reasons, steps and/or slide outs are not permitted to be extended overnight. Occupancy of your unit overnight in your assigned site is not allowed.

21. If a resident with a unit parked in the Boat/RV Facility sells their house prior to the completion of a new house being built or resale/purchase (i.e., becoming a non-resident), the resident may continue to park said unit in the facility. The resident will need to provide proof of a sales contract that is in force and rental site fees for the unit must be paid to cover the time period.

22. Residents are permitted to take their units to their homes per the Sun City Hilton Head Community Association’s Schedule of Fees and Community Rules.

23. If a resident plans to remove their unit for more than six consecutive months, but less than nine months, they must send a note to the Lot Manager stating their departure and return date in order to retain the space.

## UNIT TYPES AND CONDITIONS

1. Recreational Vehicles (RV) are defined as a motorized vehicle or trailer designed and manufactured with temporary living quarters for recreational camping travel or seasonal use. Units that measure more than 102 inches at their widest point will not be permitted. SUVs and vans that do not have temporary living quarters are not permitted for sites in the facility.

2. Specialty hobby trailers and utility trailers are defined as an open or closed type trailer, which has a single or double axel and does not include manufactured installed living quarters. These trailers have either a drop-down ramp or detachable ramps for entry/removal of classic/antique vehicles, motorcycles, four-wheel all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) or two-wheel off-road motor bikes as well as used for general storage or transportation.

3. Specialty hobby trailers and personal-use utility trailers are authorized to park in the Boat/RV Facility when space is available on a first-come, first-served basis. These vehicles must be equipped with their own wheels.

4. Boats shall be on trailers during the majority of the year. Empty boat trailers are not permitted, except during summer boating season, beginning May 1 and ending September 30. If a resident plans a longer time they must send a note to the Lot Manager as outlined in Rule 23 above.

5. Non-self-propelled units, (i.e. sail boats, boats, canoes, kayaks, etc.) are not permitted in the facility.

6. RVs, travel trailers and boats have priority if the facility is full and spaces are needed. Specialty hobby units and personal-use utility units will be required to vacate the facility to allow for RV and boat parking based on a last in, first out and square foot requirement. The original date of parking will be based on Community Association facility files. The order for removal will be: Utility units first and specialty hobby units second. When an occurrence arises for removal, names of all owners parked in the facility will be grouped (i.e., personal-use utility units as a group and specialty hobby units as another group); the last in, first out rule will apply per group according to the priority listed above beginning with utility units. The process will be conducted by the Community Association Management and the Lot Manager. The owners will have 15 days to vacate the facility after receiving written notification from the Community Association.

7. Members with boats may place a storage box behind their boat (space permitting). All storage boxes for the boats must be a neutral color. All storage boxes must be approved by the Lot Manager. All material and equipment shall be kept inside the boat box.

8. A limit of one type of each unit per household will be allowed into the facility: one boat, one RV/travel trailer, one specialty hobby trailer, one personal-use trailer.

## SAFETY, SECURITY AND INFORMATION

The Boat/RV Facility is unmanned. Video surveillance cameras are installed on the property for safety and security.

1. Located near the entrance to the wash station is an emergency phone and an Automated Emergency Defibrillator (AED), with voice activation and directions on how to use attached. Fire extinguishers are on most light poles throughout the facility.

2. To the right as you enter the facility is the Boat/RV bulletin board with a site listing of names and phone numbers, in addition to important information for the facility users.

3. Do not allow anyone to use your authorized activity card to gain access to the facility. The penalty for violating this rule is as follows:
    - First offense: A fine per the Community Association Schedule of Fees and Community Rules.
    - Second offense: A fine plus a 12-month suspension of parking and campsite privileges.
    - Third offense: After written notice has been received, the resident will have 15 days to remove all of their units. Failure to remove the units in a timely manner will result in towing the units from the facility at the owner’s expense.

4. Entry is only permitted by an authorized activity card. When entering the facility, stop after you are through the gate and wait until the gate is completely closed before proceeding into the facility. Once you have passed through the gate, you must stop and wait for the gate to completely close behind you. Everyone entering the facility must scan their authorized activity card and follow the proper procedures.

    **Failure to comply with entry and exit gate closure procedures may result in the same penalties as delineated in line item 3 above.**

5. Any issues with opening or closing of the gate should be reported to Security at the phone number on the gate.

6. Exit from the facility is by an authorized activity card. When exiting the facility, stop after you are through the gate, wait until the gate is fully closed before proceeding. When there is more than one vehicle leaving the facility at the same time, the first vehicle will scan their authorized activity card and pass through the gate, the other vehicles will follow the first vehicle through the gate scanning their authorized activity card and proceeding to exit. The last vehicle will stop after going through the gate and wait for the gate to fully close before proceeding.

    **Failure to comply with entry and exit gate closure procedures may result in the same penalties as delineated in line item 3 above.**

7. An authorized resident is a Sun City resident who is renting a site in the facility who wishes to bring an unauthorized vehicle (guest or i.e. repair or maintenance person) into the facility must follow this procedure:
    - Scan your authorized activity card and enter the facility.
    - Have your guest follow you through the gate.
    - The same procedure is followed upon exit from the facility.

8. Guests must be accompanied by owners at all times when inside the facility. Owners are responsible for their guest’s actions and behavior. This includes all service providers working on units.

Anyone who causes damage to any of the facilities or equipment or units will be billed for the cost of the repairs and may be assessed an appropriate fine. Anyone found to damage another unit in the facility and not report it to the owner and the Lot Manager is subject to removal from the facility and loss of their site or sites.

9. Loss of parking privileges may result in suspension from the facility for one year. A suspended resident cannot apply for another parking space for one year from the date of suspension.

The purpose of the Sun City Hilton Head Boat/RV Facility is to enhance Sun City Hilton Head residents’ active lifestyle. Residents may park their boats, RVs and trailers in the facility with easy access to their units for trips and outings. Long-term storage non-use is not allowed.

This is an active use facility; it is not a storage facility for unused units as defined by the rules and regulations stated in this document.
