# Managed Neighborhoods Sun City North & Sun City West Package

Several aspects of your landscape maintenance are provided to you by the Sun City Hilton Head Community Association through your benefit assessment fees.  Please keep in mind that the information in this packet is an outline of the included services, however, due to weather conditions, holidays or other unforeseen circumstances, some aspects of these services may change relative to frequency or when the service is provided.

 If you have questions, comments or concerns regarding your benefitted landscape services, please contact the Managed Neighborhoods Office at managed.neighborhoods@schhca.com or call (843) 705-4061; be sure to include your name, address, contact information and issue or concern.

## Submitting a Work Order Ticket

The Community Association utilizes a work ticket system for residents to submit issues pertaining to various departments such as Common Area, Facilities Maintenance and Managed Neighborhoods (MN). Relative to Managed Neighborhoods, please read below for information on what types of issues should be submitted through the work ticket system and which should not.

### Work Order Ticket Required

Please email WebHelpDesk@schhca.com to submit a work ticket with your name, phone number, your address, and the issue or concern; please attach a photo(s) to your email** as they are very helpful. Once you submit your work ticket, you’ll receive a confirmation email from the work ticket system with “[Ticket #XXXXX]” in the subject line. Please make note of the work ticket number (WT#). If you have questions or additional information to add, please reply to any email that you receive from the work ticket system that has the matching WT#. Doing so will apply your correspondence directly to the work ticket keeping all pertinent information together. You will continue to receive emails from the work ticket system anytime a note is added to your work ticket until it has been closed.

### As Needed Services

We heavily rely on a good partnership with our residents to inform us if they require an “as needed” service(s) by submitting a work ticket as each property is unique and has slightly different needs. Examples of “as needed” services: developer plant bed weed control, crack weeds, or turf condition issues (discoloration, dying, thinning, possible fungus or insects, etc.).

### Missed Service

Once it has been communicated via the Daily Update email that all services have been completed in your neighborhood*, residents may submit a work order ticket to the system informing us that your property did not receive complete services. Please ensure that you only do this AFTER reviewing the Daily Update email, as sometimes there are instances where weather or turf conditions will force the vendor to skip over specific aspects of service. Examples of missed services: grass clippings left on** patios, one side of the driveway being edged but not the other, back yard mowed, but not the front yard, etc. _*Please note that any work tickets generated prior to the Daily Update email stating that an entire neighborhood has been_ _completed will be closed._

### Damage

Any damage sustained during services provided as a part of your Sun City North & Sun City West Package should be submitted through the work ticket system. Some examples of this are: turf scalping, home or property damage caused during service, chemical burn, tire ruts**, etc.

### Items that should not be submitted to the work ticket system

Items that are not included in the Sun City North & Sun City West Package should not be submitted to the work ticket system as they are a resident responsibility. Examples of these items are as follows:
  - Drainage issues or standing water
  - Animal pest issues

  - General questions (please direct general questions regarding MN to Managed Neighborhoods@schhca.com)
  - Irrigation system problems or maintenance requests (unless damage was caused by the Association’s vendor)
  - Turf issues (lumpy turf) or turf decline due to drainage issues, previous fungus damage, root intrusion, shade, over-irrigation, etc. (unless damage was caused by the Association’s vendor)
  - Tracks, not ruts, left by mowers**
  - Tree, shrub, or other planting concerns (including the re-staking of a tree)
  - Issues regarding landscape materials installed by Pulte
  - Proposal requests for out of contract services, or any issues pertaining to additional services that you have privately contracted with our vendor 
  
_Ruts and tracks are often confused. Ruts refer to deep depressions into (typically) oversaturated turf/soil leaving a clear,_ _defined, impression of a tire (tread and outer edges of the tire), sometimes several inches, into the ground. Ruts typically_ _need some sort of intervention to repair whether tamping the earth down or replacement of the sod. Tracks are superficial marks left by tires in moist soil/turf where markings of the treads of a tire are present but lack a deep, clear impression of the outer edges of a tire._

## Landscape Maintenance

**The Sun City North & Sun City West Package includes the following items:**

Weekly lawn mowing (weather permitting) during the growing season (April – October), as needed services are provided the remainder of the year as deemed necessary by SCHHCA Managed Neighborhoods.

Edging around walkways, driveways and developer planting beds*, no less than every other visit, depending on conditions.

Clippings blown from walkways, driveways and porches/patios (not enclosed) at each mowing service.

Weed control of plant beds* (as needed.)

Up to six turf applications per year to include fertilizations, pre-emergent and insecticide. Post-emergent spread throughout the program as needed. One fungicide application, if necessary.

Two pine straw applications per year, spring and fall, which includes developer plant beds* only.

One irrigation system inspection per year (winter/early spring) to review the overall function of your system.

a. A door tag will be left at each residence denoting any issue(s) found in need of repair.

b. Only minor adjustments such as a broken head replacement will be made to the irrigation system (as a courtesy), as irrigation maintenance is a resident responsibility. _All repairs are optional. Residents may contact the vendor directly to schedule any needed repair(s) using the contact information listed on the door tag, or they may contact another vendor of their choice._

Residents can opt out of select services only and must do so during the opt out window which takes place prior to the season starting (November-December). Flags will be delivered to your home by the landscape provider prior to the first application or service.

**Red – Denotes no turf applications**  
**Blue - Denotes no irrigation system inspection**

Flags denoting opt-outs will be placed in the yard and must remain where they are placed to ensure visibility. Red flags must remain the entire season. Blue flags can be removed once the service has concluded in the entire neighborhood.

There is no reduction in costs or materials for residents who voluntarily opt-out of service(s). Opt-out provisions exclusively apply to turf applications, irrigation system and pine straw applications.

Residents may not opt-out of the fungicide and insecticide applications as an infestation may directly affect the entire neighborhood or community.

_Developer (installed) plant beds refers to the front planting bed at the foundation of the home, utility plant beds and front yard tree ring._

## Turf Application Schedule

### Agronomic Program Details: Bermuda and Centipede

#### February/March (application 1):

- Granular pre-emergent application and “Poly-On” polymer coated slow-release fertilizer         (activated when surface temperature reaches 55 degrees, continuous feed through August)
- Fertilizer ratio 3-1-2 (based on current conditions prior to application)
- Nitrogen application is based on conditions annually

#### April/May (applications 2 and 3):

- Insecticide application targeting mole crickets and fire ants (12 month residual effect)
- Post emergent herbicide application (grassy and broadleaf weed control)

#### June/July (application 4):

- Post emergent herbicide application (grassy and broadleaf weed control)
- Iron application (as needed)

#### August/September (application 5):

- Post emergent herbicide application (grassy and broadleaf weed control)
- Iron application (as needed)
- Insecticide application for army worm activity (as needed)

#### October (application 6):

- Granular pre-emergent application (to prevent weed growth the following season)
- Fungicide application (as needed)
 
Our vendor will use its best judgement when scheduling turf applications to minimize any potential dilution due to weather. As outlined above, dependent on various factors including timing, conditions, efficiency, or other reasons, the landscape provider may combine certain applications when applicable. The application schedule, products and methods of application may change due to conditions, temperatures, resistance and current needs of the turf species. The current program will help control weeds but cannot eliminate them entirely.

## Mandatory Applications

### Insecticide

Fipronil, referred to as Top Choice, is a systemic and contact insecticide which will be applied as warranted throughout the entire community. This product will target specific pests that damage turf such as: mole crickets, army worms and fire ants. The timeliness of the application will be at the suggestion of the contractor. Additional applications of insecticides may be necessary throughout the growing season.

### Fungicide

A fungicide (product TBD) with systemic and contact qualities will be applied to contain fungus and provide a 21-28-day residual. This is a one-time application covered under the contract. If additional applications are required, it will be at the expense of the homeowner. BrightView can provide additional applications upon requests through their office. BrightView will only apply this product if there are signs of fungus emerging “naturally” throughout the community. The targeted fungus is “brown patch.” If necessary, communication will be sent ahead of time as to the date of the application.

**Dates will be provided prior to each application. Products may vary due to weather conditions. The turf application schedule is weather dependent and permitting.**

## Irrigation Repairs

All aspects of irrigation systems, including adjustments and programming, are the responsibility of the homeowner and not included under maintenance. To request repair services, please contact BrightView directly at suncityhhwt@brightview.com. Residents may also hire an outside irrigation contractor of their choice.

BrightView will accept responsibility for damages caused to irrigation systems during lawn care services by submitting a work order ticket as outlined previously in this package.
