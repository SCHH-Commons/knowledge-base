# Managed Neighborhood Villa and Duet Package

Villa and Duet ownership includes benefits which are provided to you by the Sun City Hilton Head Community Association as a result of the assessment fees that you pay. The Managed Neighborhoods Office manages and works in partnership with several vendors to assure delivery of these services as outlined in this package.

 Please keep in mind that the information in this packet is an outline of the included services, however, due to weather conditions, holidays or other unforeseen circumstances, some aspects of these services may change relative to the frequency and/or when the service is provided.

 If you have questions, comments or concerns regarding your benefited landscape maintenance, please contact the Managed Neighborhoods Office at
 managed.neighborhoods@schhca.com or call (843) 705-4061. Include your name, address, contact information, and issue or concern.

## Submitting a Work Order Ticket

The Community Association utilizes a work ticket system for residents to submit issues pertaining to various departments such as Common Area, Facilities Maintenance, and Managed Neighborhoods (MN). Relative to Managed Neighborhoods please read below for information on what types of issues should be submitted through the work ticket system and which should not.

### Work Order Ticket Required

Please email WebHelpDesk@schhca.com to submit a work ticket with your name, phone number, your address, and the issue or concern; please attach a photo(s) to your email as they are very helpful. Once you submit your work ticket, you’ll receive a confirmation email from the work ticket system with “[Ticket #XXXXX]” in the subject line. Please make note of the work ticket number (WT#). If you have questions or additional information to add, please reply to any email that you receive from the work ticket system that has the matching WT#. Doing so will apply your correspondence directly to the work ticket keeping all pertinent information together. You will continue to receive emails from the work ticket system anytime a note is added to your work ticket until it has been closed.

### As Needed Services

We heavily rely on a good partnership with our residents to inform us if they require** an “as needed” service(s) by submitting a work ticket as each property is unique and has slightly different needs. Examples of “as needed” services: developer plant bed weed control, crack weeds, or turf condition issues (discoloration, dying, thinning, possible fungus or insects, etc.), or roofing issues/possible leaks. Roof concerns should be submitted to the work ticket system as soon as they’re discovered. If the issue or leak is substantial, please be sure to call MN at (843) 705-4061 in addition to submitting the work ticket.

### Missed Service

Once it has been communicated via the Daily Update email that all services have been completed in your neighborhood*, residents may submit a work order ticket to the system informing us that your property did not receive complete services. Please ensure that you only do this AFTER reviewing the Daily Update email, as sometimes there are instances where weather or turf conditions will force the vendor to skip over specific aspects of service. Examples of missed services: grass clippings left on patios, one side of the driveway being edged but not the other, back yard mowed, but not the front yard, etc.

### Damage

Any damage sustained during services provided as a part of your Villa and Duet Package should be submitted through the work ticket system. Some examples of this are: turf scalping, home or property damage caused during servicing, foliage chemical burn, tire ruts**, etc.

### Items that should NOT be submitted to the work ticket system

Items that are not included in the Villa and Duet Package should not be submitted to the work ticket system as they are a resident responsibility. Examples of these items are as follows:
- Animal pest issues
- Irrigation controller replacement request or other irrigation items that are not covered
- Pop up, French, or other (personal) drain system repair or clean-out
- Turf issues/decline due to drainage issues, root intrusion, shade, etc. (unless caused by the Association’s vendor)
- Tracks (not ruts) left by mowers**
- Miscellaneous home repairs such as siding, HVAC, wood rot, walkways, etc.
- Replacement requests for foliage that has declined, or died, due to reaching its’ life expectancy
- Individual requests for roof blow offs, power washing, etc. (Please direct questions regarding these items to your NR.)
- Proposal requests for out of contract services, or any issues pertaining to additional services that you have privately contracted with our vendor
- Pruning requests immediately following a plant growth regulator (PGR) application
- Modification Requests (all requests of this nature should be directed to the Modifications Dept.)

_**Ruts and tracks are often confused. Ruts refer to deep depressions into soft or oversaturated turf/soil leaving a clear,_ _defined, impression of the tread & outer edges of the tire, up to several inches, into the ground. Ruts typically need_ _intervention to repair whether tamping the earth down or replacement sod. Tracks are superficial tire marks left by tires on_ _the soil/turf but lack a deep, clear, impression of the outer edges of a tire._

## Landscape Maintenance Scope of Work

### General Maintenance

This includes the maintenance of all Exclusive Common Area in Villa and Duet neighborhoods as well as privately owned lots in cottage neighborhoods.

To include: 

- Turf mowing occurs once a week (weather permitting) during the growing season (approximately April through October), and as needed for the remainder of the year
- Edging around walkways, driveways and plant beds on a rotational, biweekly, basis
- Debris removal after pruning or trimming takes place. Debris cut by residents will not be removed
- Weed removal using mechanical, chemical or manual methods
- Rejuvenation of plants with hard pruning as necessary
- Pruning low-hanging branches on trees

### Irrigation

All aspects of irrigation systems are maintained, which include adjustments, programming and repair as needed.

Items financially covered under the contract: after hours emergency calls, backflow troubleshooting, general sprinkler head and lateral line work, non-pressurized lines repair (from valve in), rain sensors, sprinkler heads and nozzles, valve boxes and covers and wiring tracing.

Items not financially covered under the contract: backflows, clocks/controller and pedestals, pressurized mainline, electrical and mechanical valves

### Horticultural Applications

Turf fertilization twice a year (spring and fall)

Fungicide and insecticide as needed to control disease, fungus and insects

Pre-emergent and selective post-emergent herbicide for turf weeds

Plant material fertilization twice a year (spring and fall)

Ornamental trees (including palm trees), fertilized as needed

### Other

Uniform pruning - no special requests for pruning (i.e. height, to do/not to do, privacy etc.)

Replacement of dead plant material and turf to occur during certain times of the year relative to environmental conditions at the discretion of SCHHCA Managed Neighborhoods.

Pine straw application occurs twice a year (spring and fall

### Landscape Maintenance Schedule

During the growing season, the Managed Neighborhoods Office sends the NRs & ANRs a daily update email which includes detailed information, for them to share with their neighbors, regarding the current service schedule.

During the dormant season, pertinent updates are shared via email with NRs & ANRs, as necessary, who will then distribute these service updates to their neighbors.

A condensed version of the same information provided to the NRs/ANRs will also be posted to the Buzz Line at (843) 645-4517. The recording is updated each weekday, Monday-Friday
