# SCHH Pickleball Club

### SCHH Pickleball Club – Mixed Doubles Ladder League

**Type:** Sports League
**Start Date:** 2025-11-01
**End Date:** 2025-12-31
**Organizer:** SCHH Pickleball Club Competitive Play Committee
**Contact:** Steve Lerner — [steve.lerner@icloud.com](mailto:steve.lerner@icloud.com)

**Details:**
The SCHH Pickleball Club Competitive Play Committee announces the launch of a **Mixed Doubles Ladder League** beginning **November 1**. The first session runs through the end of December, with session winners recognized at that time. If participation is strong, additional sessions will follow quarterly.

---

#### Format and Seeding

* Initial ladder seeding is based on **combined DUPR ratings**.
* All match results will be reported to **DUPR**.
* A team’s **combined rating** = both partners’ DUPR scores (e.g., *3.3 + 3.4 = 6.7 team rating*).
* If one partner is **not rated (NR)**, the rated player’s DUPR will be used for both players (e.g., *3.2 + NR = 6.4*).
* **Entry deadline for initial seeding:** October 30.
* Teams joining after October 30 will start at the **bottom of their division**, regardless of rating.

---

#### Divisions

* **Division 1:** Combined DUPR 7.0 and above
* **Division 2:** Combined DUPR below 7.0
  *(No minimum or maximum number of teams per division.)*

---

#### Match Play and Challenges

* Teams may **challenge up to two positions** above them on the ladder.
* Matches are **best 2 out of 3 games to 11 (win by 2)**.
* If the challenging team wins, they **take the higher team’s position**.
* Teams schedule matches directly with opponents using provided email addresses.
* **Report match results** promptly to [Steve Lerner](mailto:steve.lerner@icloud.com) after each match.

---

#### League Play Expectations

* Teams are encouraged to play **at least one match per week**.
* Teams may play **as many matches as desired** during the session.
* The team holding the **top position at the end** of the session will be recognized as the **division winner**.

---

#### Registration and Fees

* The ladder will be tracked on a **shared Google Sheet**:
  [Ladder Tracking Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1zXZeqkHwh4BX78A3fFJxDzOOCMkqHmY4K7hs6JonxeE/edit?usp=sharing)
* All match results will be updated there and submitted to DUPR.
* Both players must have an **active DUPR account** before entering.
* **Entry fee:** $10 per player, charged to your CAM card.
* Both partners must register using the **entry form** for the team to be added to the ladder:
  [Entry Form](https://forms.gle/3SuVUneRwPn74EpNA)
