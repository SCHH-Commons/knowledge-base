### Common Area Treatment to Turf

**Type:** Maintenance Notice
**Date:** October 2025 (weather permitting)
**Details:** Pre-emergent and insecticide will be applied to turf in common areas during October, weather permitting. Residents and pets should remain off treated areas for 48 hours. Signs will be posted in treated areas.

### Stay Prepared During Hurricane Season

**Type:** Safety Reminder
**Details:** It’s prime time for hurricane activity—make sure you’re prepared! Visit the [Hurricane Awareness page](#) of the community website to help make hurricane preparedness plans.
Residents are encouraged to sign up for Everbridge Nixle alerts from local officials by texting their zip code to **888777** or by downloading the **Everbridge app** in your phone’s app store.

### Pickleball Court Closures

**Type:** Facilities Update
**Details:** A resurfacing project is in progress on the pickleball courts. Residents should check the posted court schedule to see which courts are closed, as the schedule may change. The project is expected to be completed in four weeks, weather permitting.
