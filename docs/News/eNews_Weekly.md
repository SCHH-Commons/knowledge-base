# SCHH eNews Weekly

### Board Appointed Committee Applications

**Type:** Volunteer Opportunity
**Deadline:** Friday, October 24
**Details:**
The Community Association is seeking volunteers to serve on Board Appointed Committees (BAC) for the term beginning January 1, 2026. Visit the **[Board Appointed Committee page](#)** of the community website to learn the purpose and responsibilities of each of the committees.

Serving on a BAC is an excellent way to support your community and Board of Directors while meeting new neighbors.

### Stay Prepared During Hurricane Season

**Type:** Safety Reminder
**Details:**
We are in prime time for hurricane activity. Visit the **[Hurricane Awareness page](#)** to review hurricane preparedness plans.

Residents are encouraged to:

* Sign up for Everbridge Nixle alerts by texting their zip code to 888777
* Download the Everbridge app in their phone’s app store
