# Around the Lowcountry

The beauty of living in Sun City Hilton Head is the access to all this coastal region has to offer. If you're looking for outdoor activities, delicious dining options, quality shopping and fun day trips within a short drive, be sure to check out Bluffton and nearby Hilton Head Island, Beaufort and Savannah, Georgia.

## Bluffton

Explore the historic district of Old Town Bluffton and the Promenade, offering a variety of locally owned shops, restaurants and breweries. Don’t miss the views of the beautiful May River and the Church of the Cross on Calhoun Street, and check out the Bluffton Oyster Company, where oysters are still shucked by hand. Be sure to stop by the Heyward House, the town’s official Welcome Center, and learn about the town’s rich history.

## Hilton Head Island

A short drive east of Sun City will take you to Hilton Head Island, offering multiple beach options and endless walking, biking and water sports opportunities. The Sea Pines Resort, home to the popular Heritage Golf Tournament, also has a lighthouse and shopping. Residents love visiting the island for a delicious meal with a view at a variety of restaurants, and even catching a glimpse of dolphins on a sunset boat tour.

## Beaufort

West of Sun City, residents will find the historic town of Beaufort, home to beautiful colonial-style homes, shopping and the famous Waterfront. Take a carriage ride through town or check out local sites such as the Beaufort National Cemetery, Spanish Moss Trail and the Santa Elena Museum. Beaufort is also close to neighboring islands like Lady’s Island and Hunting Island.

## Savannah

Known as the Hostess City, Savannah, Georgia always makes for a fun-filled day trip. Walk the length of River Street with a view of the Savannah River and Talmadge Bridge, take a bus tour to learn about the historic city, walk through Bonaventure Cemetery and view the spectacular fountain at Forsyth Park. Tybee Island is also a short drive from Savannah, offering pristine beaches, a historic lighthouse and dining options.
