# Pools  

### Outdoor Pool Hours of Operation 

Hidden Cypress and Lakehouse outdoor pools are open 6 a.m.–10 p.m. daily.  
Barataria and Riverbend outdoor pools are open 6:30 a.m.–8:30 p.m. daily. 

### Indoor Pool Hours of Operation

Indoor pools are open from 5 a.m.–9 p.m. daily. 

**Exclusive Use Hours**   
The Purrysburg indoor pool is closed for exclusive use by the Health and Wellness Department’s water group fitness classes on:  
         • Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 8–10 a.m.  
         • Tuesday and Thursday from 9–10 a.m.  
         • Every other Saturday from 8–9 a.m.  

Lap lanes are not available for swimmers or water walkers at these times. 

**Designated Use Hours for Clubs**   

**Purrysburg Indoor Pool**  
Certain areas of the Purrysburg indoor pool are closed for club use at the times listed below:  

**Synchronettes**  
           • Wednesdays 1–3 p.m., Open Area only  
           • Saturdays 9–11:30 a.m., Entire Pool   

**Indoor Pool Volleyball**   
         • Mondays, 5–7 p.m., Open Area only  
         • Saturdays, 5–7 p.m., Open Area only  

**Lakehouse Indoor Pool**  
The Lakehouse indoor pool is closed for exclusive use by the Swim Club on:  
         • Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 12:30–4 p.m.  
  
Lap lanes are not available for swimmers or water walkers at these times.  

### Children’s Pool Hours

**Call the automated Pool Hotline at (843) 645-4518 for a recording of the current children’s pool hours.** 

Children's pool hours are 11 a.m.–5 p.m. at rotating outdoor pools according to the schedule below.

Monday: Hidden Cypress  
Tuesday: Lakehouse   
Wednesday: Riverbend  
Thursday: Barataria   
Friday: Hidden Cypress  
Saturday: Lakehouse   
Sunday: Barataria  

Between October 1–March 31, children's pool hours are also available at the Purrysburg indoor pool from 3–5 p.m. daily.
