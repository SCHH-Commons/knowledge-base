# Town Square

From performing arts to photography, world-class entertainment to hand-made crafts, Sun City Hilton Head’s Town Square is a hub for hobbies and socializing. Town Square is a collection of amenity buildings located on Sun City Lane and Del Webb Boulevard.

## Magnolia Hall

Sun City Hilton Head is proud to host a variety of exciting entertainment year-round at the community’s premier entertainment venue, Magnolia Hall. This beautiful 550-seat auditorium is the site for movie showings, theater and dance productions, concerts and large meetings. See Lifestyle Services for upcoming shows at Magnolia Hall and check out Chartered Club News in SunSations for more information about Sun City’s performing arts clubs.

## Pinckney Hall

This multi-purpose building is designed to serve the social and entertainment needs of residents. Aside from meeting space, Pinckney Hall houses a ballroom and a Broadcast Studio.

## Yemassee Crafts Center

Located in Town Square, the Yemassee Crafts Center provides meeting rooms and specialized space for a wide range of clubs and groups including art and ceramics studios, sewing, photography, computer, cards and games, billiards and table tennis rooms. See Chartered Club News in SunSations for information on the community’s many hobby and arts clubs.
