### Managed Neighborhood
 Cottage Package


-----

###### Cottage ownership includes benefits which are provided to you by the Sun City Hilton Head Community Association as a result of the assessment fees that you pay. The Managed Neighborhoods Office manages and works in partnership with several vendors to assure delivery of these services as outlined in this package.

 Please keep in mind that the information in this packet is an outline of the included services, however, due to weather conditions, holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances, some aspects of these services may change relative to the frequency or when the service is provided.

 If you have questions, comments or concerns regarding your benefited landscape maintenance, please contact the Managed Neighborhoods Office at 
 managed.neighborhoods@schhca.com or call (843) 705-4061. Include your name, address, contact information, and issue or concern.

 Information regarding the following items is included in this package:

 1) Assessment Overview/Responsibility Chart 2) Submitting a Work Order Ticket 3) Landscape Maintenance Scope of Work

 • Argent Place (29 units)

###### (Cottages)


The Community Association utilizes a work ticket system for residents to submit issues pertaining to various
departments such as Common Area, Facilities Maintenance and Managed Neighborhoods (MN). Relative to
Managed Neighborhoods, please read below for information on what types of issues should be submitted
through the work ticket system and which should not.

###### Work Order Ticket Required: Please email WebHelpDesk@schhca.com to submit a work ticket with
**your name, phone number, your address, and the issue or concern; when possible, please attach a photo(s)**
to your email as they are very helpful. Once you submit your work ticket, you’ll receive a confirmation email from
the work ticket system with “[Ticket #XXXXX]” in the subject line. Please make note of the work ticket number
**(WT#). If you have questions or additional information to add, please reply to any email that you receive from**
**the work ticket system that has the matching WT#. Doing so will apply your correspondence directly to the**
work ticket keeping all pertinent information together. You will continue to receive emails from the work ticket
system anytime a note is added to your work ticket until it has been closed.

**As Needed Services: We heavily rely on a good partnership with our residents to inform us if they require an**
“as needed” service(s) by submitting a work ticket as each property is unique and has slightly different needs.
Examples of “as needed” services: developer plant bed weed control, crack weeds, or turf condition issues
(discoloration, dying, thinning, possible fungus or insects, etc.).

**Missed Service: Once it has been communicated via the Daily Update email that all services have been**
completed in your neighborhood*, residents may submit a work order ticket to the system informing us that your
property did not receive complete services. Please ensure that you only do this AFTER reviewing the Daily
**Update email, as sometimes there are instances where weather or turf conditions will force the vendor to**
**skip over specific aspects of service. Examples of missed services: grass clippings left on patios, one side of**
the driveway being edged but not the other, back yard mowed, but not the front yard, etc.

**Damage: Any damage sustained during services provided as a part of your Cottage Package should be**
submitted through the work ticket system. Some examples of this are: turf scalping, home or property damage
caused during service, foilage chemical burn, tire ruts**, etc.

###### Items that should not be submitted to the work ticket system: Items that are not included in the
Cottage Package should not be submitted to the work ticket system as they are a resident responsibility.
Examples of these items are as follows:

  - Animal pest issues

  - Irrigation controller replacement request or other irrigation items that are not covered

  - Pop up, French, or other (personal) drain system repair or clean-out

  - Turf issues/decline due to drainage issues, root intrusion, shade, etc. (unless caused by the Association’s vendor)

  - Tracks (not ruts) left by mowers**

  - Miscellaneous home repairs such as siding, HVAC, wood rot, walkways, etc.

  - Replacement requests for foliage that has declined or died due to reaching its’ life expectancy

  - Individual requests for roof blow offs, power washing, etc. (Please direct questions regarding these items to your NR.)

  - Proposal requests for out of contract services or any issues pertaining to additional services that you have

privately contracted with our vendor

  - Pruning requests immediately following a plant growth regulator (PGR) application

  - Modification Requests (all requests of this nature should be directed to the Modifications Dept.)

_**Ruts and tracks are often confused. Ruts refer to deep depressions into (typically) oversaturated turf/soil leaving a clear,_
_defined, impression of a tire (tread and outer edges of the tire), sometimes several inches, into the ground. Ruts typically need_
_some sort of intervention to repair whether tamping the earth down or replacement of the sod. Tracks are superficial marks left_
