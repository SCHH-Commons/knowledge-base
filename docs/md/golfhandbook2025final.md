Sun City Hilton Head offers a wide range of golf and fellowship opportunities for
residents who play occasionally as well as those who wish to play every day. In
addition to individual tee times, which can be booked online, there are a number
of golf associations for men, women, couples, and 9- and 18-hole players. Review
the Chartered Club listings in SunSations and on the community website for more
information.

Resident golf plans are available to fit a variety of needs, and a United States Golf
Association (USGA) handicap can be established through the SCHH Golf Club. You
will be able to post scores to update your handicap online.

This handbook provides general information and guidance for resident and
guest use of the Sun City Hilton Head golf facilities. Course policies and golfer
responsibilities are addressed under individual topic headings.

Golfers must abide by the Golf Course General Rules and Proper Golf Etiquette
as defined in the Schedule of Fees and Community Rules. Failure to comply may
result in fines and loss of golf privileges. The Community Association shall apply
and enforce the Community Rules with members and residents equally.

A resident tee time may be reserved via the online reservation system
(Chelsea) at http://hilthead.chelseareservations.com or by calling the golf shop
up to three days in advance. New residents must visit one of the golf shops to
create a Chelsea account. Note: Chelsea reservation details are available in each
_golf shop._

**II. REGISTRATION AND CHECK-IN**
All golfers and their guests must check in with the golf shop prior to
commencing play. Golfers are encouraged to arrive and check in with the
bag drop or golf shop staff prior to their scheduled tee time. However, golfers
must check in at least 15 minutes prior to their scheduled tee time to avoid
possible loss of tee time and/or a cancellation charge.

**III. CANCELATIONS**
Reserved tee times may be canceled by emailing or calling the golf shop
at least two hours prior to the scheduled tee time or logging into Chelsea
reservation system up until 6 a.m. on the day of play. Golfers who do not
check in or cancel a reservation are subject to a no-show fee.

Please note that it is each golfer’s responsibility to cancel his/her own tee
time. Relying on another person to cancel for you puts you at risk of penalty.
The no-show fee applies to 9-hole or 18-hole reservations at any course; golf
plan or green fee amounts will not be a factor. The golf shops will keep the
Chelsea tee times live until 6 a.m. on the day of play. This change enables
a golfer to log into Chelsea to cancel or add his/her tee time reservation
overnight. The golf shops will continue to accept tee time cancelations by
phone and email. However, it is preferred to cancel via Chelsea or email
because these methods provide timestamps, which is important for early
morning tee time cancellations.

The same rules apply to guests of residents, and the no-show fee will be
charged to the host resident’s activity card account. On days where the
courses close due to unplayable conditions, the cancelation/no-show policy
will be waived. On full cart path only days, golfers should still notify the golf
shop of their cancelation however the fee for not doing so is waived

**V. DRESS CODE**
Sun City Hilton Head golf courses maintain and enforce dress standards
consistent with quality golf courses in the area. All residents and guests
(including juniors) must conform to the dress standards stated below at all
times. Residents and guests not conforming to dress code standards will be
asked to make the necessary changes before playing or using practice facilities.

**Gentlemen/Boys: Collared shirts, turtlenecks, mock turtlenecks, Bermuda/golf**
shorts or slacks.

**Ladies/Girls: Collared shirts, sleeveless blouses with or without collar,**
Bermuda/golf shorts of reasonable length, crop pants and/or slacks, covered
leggings and dresses.

**Shoes: Golf shoes with soft spikes (including golf sandals) or athletic shoes are**
required. Metal spikes are not allowed on any Sun City golf courses.

**Prohibited: Denim and yoga apparel, gym shorts, sweat pants, tank tops, swim**
suits, leggings without a cover and related attire are not acceptable on the golf
courses or practice areas.

experience for the golfing public.

Golfers with qualifying disabilities that limit their ability to walk from their
golf cart to the teeing ground and putting areas may obtain a Blue Handicap
Flag at the time of check-in from the bag drop staff. Presentation of a state
issued ID card or valid automobile handicap placard to verify the need for an
accommodation must be made in the golf shop. The blue flag, prominently
displayed while driving, allows the golf cart to be driven closer (but not onto)
the teeing areas, bunkers and putting surfaces. The golf staff have placed a
blue and white pole at each green; this is the required parking spot for the
blue flag carts. No other areas around the greens may be used and carts are
asked to return to the nearest cart path when exiting. These posts will be
moved periodically to ensure quality turf conditions. Failure to abide by the
**blue flag cart operating guidelines will lead to further action by golf staff and a**
**fine. See the Schedule of Fees and Community Rules for more information.**

For golfers with exceptional disabilities, a Solorider golf cart or other
approved cart of similar design and specifications may be used closer than
the blue and white pole and may be driven onto the teeing ground and
putting areas. The daily use blue flag must be returned to the bag drop
staff upon completion of play. Blue flag and Solorider cart privileges are not
allowed on cart path only days or holes. Private blue flags are allowed but
must be registered with the golf shop at the beginning of each year and a
valid state issued ID or automobile handicap placard must be presented to
verify the need for an accommodation.

**X. PRACTICE FACILITIES**
Range balls and baskets are the property of the facility and may not be
removed from the practice areas for any reason.

Range plans are not transferable and plan holders may not share balls.

All practice facilities, driving ranges, putting greens and short game areas are
non-smoking at all times. Playing music is not allowed in these areas.

Golf instruction on the practice facilities is limited to Sun City Hilton Head golf
staff only. See the Schedule of Fees and Community Rules for further details.

**My 12-year-old grandson likes to play golf with me when he visits. Is it**
**acceptable for him to drive the golf cart while on the course?**
No. Only licensed drivers are allowed to operate golf carts within Sun City,
including on golf courses. Please see the Community Rules for additional details.

**How many individuals may occupy a golf cart while on the course?**
Only two individuals are allowed to occupy a cart while on the course. This is
for the safety of passengers traveling on uneven surfaces.

**What is the Golf Advisory Committee?**
The Golf Advisory Committee is a Board Appointed Committee made up of
resident volunteers who provide evaluation, assistance and recommendations
to the Board of Directors as necessary on all matters relating to golf.

**How do I establish a handicap?**
Membership may be obtained by joining the SCHH Golf Club.
Visit www.schhgolfclub.com/membership for more information.

**Can I walk on the golf course at any time when not playing golf?**
This is strictly prohibited by the Community Rules and Governing Documents.


**Are there discounted rates for residents?**
Yes, there are several plans available that one may select from to fit your needs.
Visit any golf shop for more details.

**I am new to Sun City Hilton Head. How do I get into the “swing of things?”**
Sun City Hilton Head has several organizations for men, women, couples, and
9- and 18-hole play. Ask a golf shop staff member about these clubs or see the
Chartered Club News section of SunSations and at the community website.

**Can I get golf instruction at our courses?**
Group clinics as well as individual instruction are available at each golf course.

**The ranger keeps telling me I need to speed up. I want to play at my own**
**pace.**
Keep in mind that one slow group delays every foursome behind them and
reduces their enjoyment of the game. Golf staff routinely monitors pace for
the enjoyment of all players and may ask you to skip a hole to place you in to
the correct position on the course.

**Can I walk the course when playing golf?**
Walking on the courses is allowed, however, in order to maintain pace of play,
others have found walking is not practical due to the length between greens
and tees.

**I just moved into our neighborhood and want to start a new golf group.**
**How do I get this started?**
Talk with the golf professional staff.
