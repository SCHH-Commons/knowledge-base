# SCHH Landscape Glossary & Explanations

**Landscape Services**

 **Service**

`o` **This is referred to a scheduled visit to perform any or all maintenance services.**
 **Mowing**

`o` **Mowing can be referred to mowing of entire lawn.**
`o` **Selective mowing means that certain areas may be mowed and others may not.**
**Circumstances to ‘not mow’ can include: areas that are too wet, recently installed sodded,**
**or areas requested not to be mowed.**
 **Hard Edging**

`o` **This is performed with every other mowing service. Areas will include: sidewalks,**
**driveways, and patios.**
`o` **A stick edger with a blade is used for this tasks.**
 **Soft Edging**

`o` **This is performed along landscape bed edges (mulch or pine needles) to keep a clean cut**
**delineation between turf and bed. It also helps control encroaching turf from invading the**
**bed.**
`o` **This is performed with a line trimmer or ‘weedeater’. Plastic line is used.**
 **Bed Weeding**

`o` **This is performed in ‘developer installed’ beds only. Meaning, these are the original beds in**
**front of your home that were installed at the time of construction.**
`o` **Performed either by hand removal or by chemical control.**
 **Crack Weed Removal**

`o` **This is referred to weeds that are growing within the expansion joints of concrete;**
**sidewalks and driveways.**
`o` **The removal and control process is performed by use of a line trimmer and by chemical**
**use.**
 **Blowing**

`o` **Blowing is performed after every mowing service and on occasion when mowing doesn’t**
**occur. Areas include: sidewalks, porches, patios, and driveways.**
`o` **A backpack blower is utilized for this task.**

**Landscape Issues**

 **Turf Scalping**

`o` **Defined as mowing down to bare dirt.**
`o` **This can have several culprits, for example:**

     - **Uneven blades on mower**

     - **Uneven topography in lawn**

     - **Straddling drainage easements**

     - **Mowing around utilities where boxes/ lids are above grade**
`o` **Solutions**

     - **Allow the turf to repair on its own. Typical timeline for repair: 2-3 weeks.**

     - **If necessary, re-sod the damaged areas.**
 **Turf Ruts**

`o` **This is where the mower wheels sink into saturated soil leaving a depression in the lawn.**
`o` **Solution**

     - **Fill in with soil and re-sod**
 **Turf Tracking**

`o` **This is very typical and normal especially when there are periods of rain and mowing**
**operations occur.**
`o` **The ‘tracking’ is caused by dirt being picked up by the mower wheel and left on top of the**
**grass.**
`o` **Does not cause any long term damage.**
`o` **Solution**

     - **Wash off tracks and allow the area to dry.**
 **Turf Insects: Mole Crickets, Army Worms, etc.**

`o` **This is an ecological issue that is not guaranteed each year.**
`o` **These can be treated with a ‘contact’ insecticide which will kill the insects instantly.**
`o` **‘Systemic’ insecticides can be used to help provide residual protection against any potential**
**insect issue.**
 **Turf Fungus (Brown Patch –most common)**

`o` **Humidity, Heat, and Rain are contributing factors to fungus forming.**
`o` **Centipede turf is the most susceptible turf to have fungus during the growing season.**
`o` **Solutions**

     - **Apply monthly preventative fungicide treatments to help safeguard your turf.**

     - **Summit will apply a 1x application per year, if needed, to all turf areas affected by**

**fungus. But does not include spot treatments or individual service. Summit will**
**apply by treating all lawns.**

     - **Apply fungicides immediately upon detection. If you treat immediately, this will**

**help stop the fungus from spreading to more areas in your lawn.**
