###### (Villas and Duets)

The Community Association utilizes a work ticket system for residents to submit issues pertaining to various
departments such as Common Area, Facilities Maintenance, and Managed Neighborhoods (MN). Relative
to Managed Neighborhoods please read below for information on what types of issues should be submitted
through the work ticket system and which should not.

###### Work Order Ticket Required: Please email WebHelpDesk@schhca.com to submit a work ticket with
**your name, phone number, your address, and the issue or concern; please attach a photo(s) to your email**
as they are very helpful. Once you submit your work ticket, you’ll receive a confirmation email from the work
ticket system with “[Ticket #XXXXX]” in the subject line. Please make note of the work ticket number (WT#).
If you have questions or additional information to add, please reply to any email that you receive from the
**work ticket system that has the matching WT#. Doing so will apply your correspondence directly to the**
work ticket keeping all pertinent information together. You will continue to receive emails from the work ticket
system anytime a note is added to your work ticket until it has been closed.

**As Needed Services: We heavily rely on a good partnership with our residents to inform us if they require**
an “as needed” service(s) by submitting a work ticket as each property is unique and has slightly different
needs. Examples of “as needed” services: developer plant bed weed control, crack weeds, or turf condition
issues (discoloration, dying, thinning, possible fungus or insects, etc.), or roofing issues/possible leaks. Roof
concerns should be submitted to the work ticket system as soon as they’re discovered. If the issue or leak is
substantial, please be sure to call MN at (843) 705-4061 in addition to submitting the work ticket.

**Missed Service: Once it has been communicated via the Daily Update email that all services have been**
completed in your neighborhood*, residents may submit a work order ticket to the system informing us that
your property did not receive complete services. Please ensure that you only do this AFTER reviewing the
**Daily Update email, as sometimes there are instances where weather or turf conditions will force the**
**vendor to skip over specific aspects of service. Examples of missed services: grass clippings left on patios,**
one side of the driveway being edged but not the other, back yard mowed, but not the front yard, etc.

**Damage: Any damage sustained during services provided as a part of your Villa and Duet Package should**
be submitted through the work ticket system. Some examples of this are: turf scalping, home or property
damage caused during servicing, foliage chemical burn, tire ruts**, etc.

###### Items that should NOT be submitted to the work ticket system: Items that are not included
in the Villa and Duet Package should not be submitted to the work ticket system as they are a resident
responsibility. Examples of these items are as follows:

  - Animal pest issues

  - Irrigation controller replacement request or other irrigation items that are not covered

  - Pop up, French, or other (personal) drain system repair or clean-out

  - Turf issues/decline due to drainage issues, root intrusion, shade, etc. (unless caused by the Association’s

vendor)

  - Tracks (not ruts) left by mowers**

  - Miscellaneous home repairs such as siding, HVAC, wood rot, walkways, etc.

  - Replacement requests for foliage that has declined, or died, due to reaching its’ life expectancy

  - Individual requests for roof blow offs, power washing, etc. (Please direct questions regarding these items

to your NR.)

  - Proposal requests for out of contract services, or any issues pertaining to additional services that you


have privately contracted with our vendor

_**Ruts and tracks are often confused. Ruts refer to deep depressions into soft or oversaturated turf/soil leaving a clear,_
_defined, impression of the tread & outer edges of the tire, up to several inches, into the ground. Ruts typically need_
_intervention to repair whether tamping the earth down or replacement sod. Tracks are superficial tire marks left by tires on_
_the soil/turf but lack a deep, clear, impression of the outer edges of a tire._
