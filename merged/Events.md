## Ticket Sales Information
- Tickets for all Lifestyle Services events are available online at: **[www.SunCityHiltonHead.org](http://www.SunCityHiltonHead.org)**  
- **Limits:**  
  - General admission & Magnolia Hall shows: 4 tickets per household  
  - Pinckney Hall seated events: 8 tickets per household (unless otherwise stated)  
- **Payments:**  
  - Online: credit/debit cards only  
  - Palmetto Commons: CAM card charges accepted  
- **Ticket Access:**  
  - No computer? Buy tickets at Lifestyle Services using your activity card  
  - Print tickets at home or at Lifestyle Services before the event  
- **Policies:**  
  - Online ticketing closes 1 hour before each event  
  - Walk-ins permitted if space allows (cash or activity card accepted)  
  - **No refunds** for purchased tickets

## Group Exercise Class Descriptions — 2025  
_Orange classes will generally be easier and low impact._

---

### Aqua ISO-3
Focuses on isokinetic, isometric, and isotonic movements to improve muscle strength without the strain of impact.  
Class is suitable for individuals with arthritis, osteoporosis, fibromyalgia, knee, hip, joint, or back issues.

---

### Aqua Aerobics
A mix of cardio, strength, core, and balance using body weight and water.

---

### Aqua Bliss
Mild to moderate class that mixes cardio, balance, strength, and muscle endurance, coordination, and cognitive exercises for a full energized body workout.

---

### Aqua Zumba
Known as the Zumba pool party. Integrating the Zumba formula and philosophy with traditional aqua fitness disciplines, blends it all together into a challenging water-based exercise session.

---

### Boot Camp
A multilevel interval training class mixing cardio with strength training for an upbeat workout. Designed to scale so all participants can get a great workout.  
**Please bring your own mat.**

---

### Build & Burn
Build muscle and burn calories with this full-body, strength-based workout.  
**Please bring your own mat.**

---

### Cardio Strength Interval (CSI)
Low-impact cardio workout with strength training mixed in throughout class. This will challenge your coordination and balance.  
Icing on the cake is a core tabata at the end. Standing & floor work.  
**Bring a mat.**

---

### Chair Yoga
Yoga designed for anyone very stiff, recovering or rehabbing from injury or illness, or unable to do floor work.

---

### Dance Fit
Fun 40 min. of high or low dance aerobics followed by 10 min. of strength training and stretch.  
**Please bring a mat.**

---

### enCORE
Simple moves to strengthen the core, promote a healthy back, and improve flexibility with full-body stretch. Standing & floor work.  
**Please bring your own mat.**

---

### Gentle Yoga
A gentle approach to yoga using meditation, breathing practices, and postures to build and maintain strength, flexibility, balance, and agility — 4 essential skills for independent living.  
Pose modifications and the occasional challenge are offered. Must be able to get up and down from the floor.  
**Bring your own yoga mat.**

---

### Good Bones
Incorporates structured, creative techniques to strengthen the spine, core, and muscles around the hips & wrists.  
Moderate impact, safe for those with osteoporosis and osteopenia. Movement quality helps alleviate joint pain, strengthen bones, and improve balance, posture & flexibility.  
**Standing & floor work. Please bring a mat.**

---

### Healthy Backs
This class combines stretching with flowing movements to promote back health, flexibility, and body awareness. Standing & floor work.  
**Please bring mat and small towel.**

---

### H.I.I.T.
High-Intensity Interval Training. Varying levels of intensity (hard, harder, hardest) will be shown to suit individual fitness levels.  
**Please bring a mat.**

---

### Kickboxing
Kickboxing movements and combinations to develop aerobic fitness, balance, coordination, strength & agility.  
Fun, high-energy workout including intervals of H.I.I.T. (high-intensity interval training).  
**Please bring a mat.**

---

### Living Younger
This class mixes soft flowing exercises to build balance & core strength.  
Fun to follow balance, dance & Tai Chi moves to calm your heart as we build our body.  
_Living Younger_ is the movement of happiness, stress reduction, and youthful feelings!

---

### Mat Pilates
Training regimen to work the entire body concentrating on strengthening to improve general fitness and overall well-being; emphasis on posture, balance, and flexibility.  
Standing & floor work.  
**Please bring your own mat.**

---

### Pedal & Pump
Combining 30 minutes of indoor cycling with 15–20 minutes of strength training/stretch exercises on the mat.  
Take your workout to the next level!

---

### People with Parkinson’s
Multi-tasking exercises for your brain to re-learn.  
Core work, cardio intervals, and Parkinson’s-specific “brain work” exercises that enhance the process of brain re-organization and repair.
