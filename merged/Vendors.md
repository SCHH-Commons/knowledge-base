## Methodology & Coverage Notes

- **Search queries used:** “Bluffton SC pavers patios contractor Sun City Hilton Head screen rooms”, “Hilton Head pergola installer”, “screen rooms Bluffton”, “Okatie concrete patio contractor”, “Savannah hardscape serves Bluffton”.  
- **Sources checked:** Company websites (services, service areas, contact pages), Google Maps business listings (for links/addresses), Yelp, Angi, Facebook pages, Better Business Bureau profiles, and local Hilton Head Area Home Builders Association directory.  
- **Licensing:** Where available, specific license numbers were captured (e.g., American Paving Design cites Beaufort County License #11100 on its site). South Carolina statewide license lookups (SC LLR) were not consistently matchable by company name online; numbers not posted by vendors were left as “Not listed.”  
- **Permits/HOA:** Marked “Not stated” unless the vendor’s site explicitly claims handling permits/ARB/HOA submissions (e.g., Palmetto Porches), or states obtaining permits (e.g., Quality Hardscapes & Porch Masters).  
- **Reviews:** Ratings and counts were recorded only where visible on public profiles at time of verification; where not visible due to platform login/JS, entries are marked “Unclear” with a link to the profile to verify. Themes were included only when corroborated by multiple comments or official summaries.  
- **Exclusions:** Lawn-only services, pool-only builders without separate patio/enclosure offerings, and general handymen were excluded.  
- **Known gaps / next-pass leads:** Capture exact Google star ratings and counts for each vendor; verify SC LLR license records by legal entity name; add additional vendors explicitly stating Bluffton/SCHH coverage (e.g., local deck builders that also install pergolas and screen rooms).
